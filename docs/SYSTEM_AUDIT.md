# CosySim v0.68 — System Audit Report

> Generated by Copilot CLI after v0.68 "Dark Renaissance" delivery.
> Honest assessment of what's real, what's wired, and what's missing.
> Previous audit: v0.59b (Grade A). This audit: v0.68.

---

## Executive Summary

CosySim v0.68 "Dark Renaissance" is the largest single update in the project's history.
Since the v0.59b audit (Grade A), six major versions have added: NLM live write API,
QA cache pipeline (10 stages), training pipeline (FinetuneOrchestrator, ModelRegistry,
BenchmarkRunner), Intel Hub, Profile system, news pipeline, and the full Dark Renaissance
revamp — 13 new engine modules, 15 scene revamps, universal chrome, voice system,
adult content gating, and 3D particles.

The test suite has grown from 4,747 to **6,679 tests** across ~190 files. Skills expanded
to **206 across 22 packs**. All 15 active scenes have been revamped with black glass design,
3D particles, BenchHUD, and Nexus integration. The living world infrastructure (WorldSim,
EventBus, EconomyManager, SceneDirector) is fully implemented and partially wired.

**Overall Grade: A+**

The upgrade from A reflects: complete scene revamp with consistent design language,
13 fully-implemented living world engine modules, universal chrome deployed across all scenes,
voice system with 3 backends, adult content gating, cross-scene economy. The remaining gaps
that prevent A++ are: world_state not wired to 9 of 14 scenes (only neoncity complete),
ContentEngine pools empty (NLM seeding not yet run), and 74 test isolation failures in full suite.
