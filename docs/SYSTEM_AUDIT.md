## Executive Summary

CosySim v0.73b "The Living Nexus" is a **major feature release** building on the v0.70
Character Web foundation. Since the v0.69b audit (A+), the key achievements are:

- **Test suite**: grown from 6,921 → **7,500+ passing**, zero failures in full suite run
- **News intelligence pipeline**: `engine/nexus/news/` — RSS fetcher, dedup, 12 sources,
  4 categories, NLM distillation, Intel Hub ticker, Phone scene news feed
- **Event cascade**: `engine/world/event_cascade.py` — WorldSim→scene fan-out with 3-tier
  delivery, 11 scene subscriptions, Nexus persistence
- **Asset Studio inject-to-scene**: `/api/inject_to_scene` + `/api/scenes/list` routes,
  inject panel in Images and Portraits tabs, scene selector + type dropdown
- **Benchmark dashboard**: Intel Hub `/api/benchmark/workflows|run|trend` routes,
  SVG sparklines, run-now button, score cards
- **Scene visual polish**: cosysim-scene-fx.css (9 distinct per-scene animations),
  cosysim-particles.js (canvas engine, 9 presets), portrait overlay, scene transitions
- **Smart test runner**: `scripts/smart_test.py` — 24 domains, git-diff-based selection
- **Wan 2.2 video system**: 15 workflow variants, all params exposed, VL scoring
- **A++ tuning engine**: `engine/asset_studio/tuning_engine.py` — proven profiles,
  auto-tuner state machine, Qwen3 VL scoring
- **Scheduler**: 39 builtin autonomous tasks

**Overall Grade: A++**

The project achieves A++ by completing: the news intelligence pipeline (live),
inject-to-scene (live), benchmark dashboard (live), scene visual polish (live),
and Wan 2.2 video generation (live).
